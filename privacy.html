Privacy Policy

ClipSpot is a personal tool operated by its owner. It uses YouTube API Services to upload short video clips to the owner's own YouTube channel, and TikTok's Content Posting API to post to the owner's own TikTok account.

Data we access and store

ClipSpot stores only the OAuth tokens needed to post to the owner's own accounts. It does not collect, sell, or share personal data from any other user, and it does not read, store, or display any other YouTube user data. Tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer and are never shared with third parties.

Google and YouTube

By using ClipSpot with YouTube you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access and deleting data

You can revoke ClipSpot's access to your Google account at any time from the Google security settings page, and to your TikTok account from the TikTok app under Settings, Security and permissions. Revoking access invalidates the stored token. Any locally stored token can also be deleted by removing the token file on the owner's computer, which deletes all data ClipSpot holds.
